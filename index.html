About Us

Cleveland Based.

Sidhu Realty Capital is a privately held, multifaceted real estate investment firm headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio. With a diverse portfolio of single tenant and mixed use retail assets across several states, we specialize in four key areas: Joint Venture Investments, Commercial Retail Real Estate, Restaurant and Hospitality, and Opportunistic Development.

We invest our own capital either independently or alongside partners through our Joint Venture Program to ensure full alignment and long term commitment to each opportunity. Whether repositioning underutilized retail assets, expanding franchise operations, or pursuing ground up development in high growth markets, our focus remains the same: unlocking value and delivering lasting returns.

The Firm

Sidhu Realty Capital is a privately held, multifaceted real estate investment firm based in Cleveland, Ohio. The firm currently invests its own money either entirely or alongside our investors in our Joint Venture Program.

This co investment model means that every transaction we pursue, we have real capital at stake alongside our partners. There is no fee driven motivation to close bad deals. Our incentives are fully aligned with yours.

Paul Sidhu
President & Chief Executive Officer

Paul Sidhu is the President and CEO of Sidhu Realty Capital. He is responsible for all strategic initiatives and oversight of the firm's activities, which encompasses both commercial real estate investments and the operation of the retail hospitality group.

Paul has over a decade of commercial real estate investment experience having managed and consulted on multi billion dollar private equity portfolios as a Portfolio Manager for UBS Investment Bank and a Senior Consultant for Deloitte.

Paul holds a Masters in Real Estate Finance from NYU's Schack Real Estate Institute, an MBA from Duke University's Fuqua School of Business, and a Bachelor of Arts from Bates College. He is a licensed real estate broker, member of ICSC, and director on MetroHealth System's Foundation Board of Directors.

Daniel Radocaj
Chief Operating & Investment Officer

As COO/CIO, Daniel oversees the administrative and operational functions of the firm while managing its financial actions and investment strategy. Daniel brings over 25 years of experience in financial, tax, business management, and consulting fields.

He has held executive leadership positions across healthcare, manufacturing, and nonprofit entities, and was named a Distinguished Hispanic Ohio Award recipient for outstanding community leadership by the Ohio Commission on Latino Affairs.

Daniel holds a Bachelor of Science and an MBA from Cleveland State University, and proudly served the country as a Sergeant in the United States Army.

James Yashnyk
Chief Strategy Officer

As Chief Strategy Officer, James directs all corporate financial and management decisions across the hospitality group. His core responsibility is to grow unit levels and consolidated P&L across the firm's restaurant and hospitality operations.

James brings 30 years of management experience in the retail, restaurant, and hospitality industry, having worked for private and publicly traded organizations including McDonald's, Einstein Bagels, The Ritz Carlton, Verizon, and T Mobile.

His career accomplishments include Manager of the Year recognition, customer service awards, and a five diamond award in hospitality, one of the most distinguished honors in the industry.

Nick Vento
General Counsel

Nick serves as General Counsel of Sidhu Realty Capital, leading the company's legal strategy and overseeing all aspects of corporate governance, compliance, litigation, and risk management. He provides counsel on commercial transactions, real estate investments, joint ventures, franchise operations, and development initiatives.

Before joining Sidhu Realty Capital, Nick practiced as a litigator at Schneider Bell in Cleveland, representing clients in complex commercial and real estate matters. He holds a Juris Doctor from Cleveland State University College of Law and a BS in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance from The Ohio State University.

Nick is admitted to practice law in Ohio and serves on the Associate Board of America SCORES Cleveland.
